## Standard vs Ultimate at a glance

| | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
|---|---|---|
| US price | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| The base game | Yes | Yes |
| 16 bonus items (vehicles, weapons, outfits, mod shops) | No | Yes |
| Physical version | Yes (download code, no disc) | No, digital only |
| Vintage Vice City Pack (bought before Nov 20, 2026) | Yes | Yes |
| Upgrade later | Ultimate Edition Upgrade, sold separately | Not needed |

Every row above is {% badge "confirmed" %} by Rockstar's [press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi) and [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions).

## Price

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 in the US. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Both editions are on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Ultimate adds 16 bonus items, including vehicles, weapons, outfits and mod shops. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar names the '95 Grotti Cheetah and the Rideout Customs Mod Shop as examples. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Rockstar hasn't published the full list of all 16 items. We'll add it once it does, or once we've seen them in-game.

## Upgrading from Standard to Ultimate

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately, at any time, on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} There's no physical Ultimate Edition. If you buy the physical Standard Edition, you can add the upgrade digitally. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Rockstar's support page doesn't list the upgrade's price. Check your console's store before you buy.

## Physical copies: a download code, not a disc

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies contain a download code. In Rockstar's words, "A disc will not be included in the box." [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies ship and are available for store pickup from November 12, 2026. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Codes redeemed before launch can pre-load from local midnight on November 12. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

So a boxed copy still means a full download. Leave room on your drive either way. Rockstar hasn't announced the file size.

## Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Vintage Vice City Pack comes with pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before November 20, 2026. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} It includes a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

You don't need to pre-order to get it. Buying on launch day, November 19, still counts.

## A free month of GTA+ (digital pre-orders)

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Digital pre-orders include one free month of GTA+, redeemable right away. It covers GTA Online perks and the GTA+ Games Library, which includes GTA V. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The PlayStation Store FAQ says the GTA+ month must be claimed by March 31, 2027. [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Rockstar ties the GTA+ month to digital pre-orders. If you buy physical and want it, check with your retailer before you order.

## Region locking

- {% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation codes are region-locked. Xbox codes aren't. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar recommends buying from a retailer in the same country as your console account. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

If you're on PS5, don't buy a cheap code from another country's store. It may not redeem on your account.

## Which edition should you buy?

This part is our opinion, not Rockstar's.

- **Most players: Standard.** It's the same game. The Ultimate extras are bonus items, and you can add them later if you want them.
- **You want a box on the shelf: physical Standard.** Just know it's a code, not a disc. Rockstar only lists the GTA+ month for digital pre-orders.
- **You know you'll want the extra cars and mod shop on day one: Ultimate.** It's digital only, so buy it from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.
- **Not sure: wait.** The Vintage Vice City Pack is yours with any purchase before November 20, so there's no penalty for buying on launch day.
