## Release date and platforms

- {% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches **November 19, 2026** on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The date has moved twice: from the original fall 2025 window to May 26, 2026 (announced May 2, 2025), then to November 19, 2026. Rockstar said the extra time was to finish the game "with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." [Rockstar Newswire, May 2025](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/258aa538o412ok/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-coming-may-26-2026), [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 2025](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/gta-6-release-delayed-november-2026-1236571679/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two reaffirmed November 19 in its May 21 and August 7, 2026 results. [Take-Two IR (May)](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fourth-2), [Take-Two IR (Aug)](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no native PS4 or Xbox One version, and the PlayStation Store FAQ says it isn't playable on PS4. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Performance:** the game is "targeting 30 frames per second" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. **PS5 Pro** adds "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro." On PS5, Rockstar also lists DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio and improved load times. [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 10](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The PlayStation Store lists the game as **PS5 Pro Enhanced**, and the Xbox Store listing is tagged "Optimized for Xbox Series X|S." [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/), [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9p3h4968grsm)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** playable on day one on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles from 1am ET on November 19. You buy the game separately, and the cloud version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings. Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/), [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two's November 9 results held the date: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away," with pre-orders "continuing to reach unprecedented levels." No pre-order count was published. [Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Unlock time:** "midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions for both PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S." [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)

Rockstar hasn't said how that works in countries with several time zones. All 15 store regions, with the US, Australia and Brazil marked Unverified, are on [GTA 6 unlock times](/release-date-times/).

## Price and editions

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Standard Edition: $79.99.** **Ultimate Edition: $99.99.** [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Ultimate Edition adds 16 bonus items, including vehicles, weapons, outfits and mod shops. Rockstar names the '95 Grotti Cheetah and the Rideout Customs Mod Shop as examples. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately at any time on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} There's no physical Ultimate Edition. Physical Standard owners can add the upgrade digitally. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies contain a download code. "A disc will not be included in the box." [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Pre-orders, pre-load and bonuses

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Pre-orders opened at midnight local time on **June 25, 2026** on the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and retailers. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Vintage Vice City Pack:** included with pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before **November 20, 2026**. It has a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Digital pre-orders include **one free month of GTA+**, redeemable right away. It covers GTA Online perks and the GTA+ Games Library, which includes GTA V. On PlayStation it must be claimed by March 31, 2027. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Pre-load is live.** It opened on Tuesday, November 10, 2026: at midnight local time on PS5 and at 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10), [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 10](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **File size:** "The pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on your device." [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Code-in-box copies are available from retailers from November 12. Codes can be activated and pre-loaded as soon as they're received. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now), [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Sony and Microsoft each say it's the most pre-ordered game in their store's history. That's each company's own claim about its own store. [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/), [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation codes are region-locked; Xbox codes aren't. Rockstar recommends buying from a retailer in the same country as your console account. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

In Rockstar's words, "an easy score goes wrong" and the pair end up in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The October 15 launch trailer shows Lucia wearing a monitoring ankle bracelet and Jason talking about life after the military. [Launch trailer](https://www.rockstargames.com/videos/76orsa95), [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/16/rockstar-releases-the-gta-6-launch-trailer-ahead-of-the-november-19-console-release/)

## Setting: Vice City and Leonida

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The game is set in the fictional US state of **Leonida**, centred on **Vice City**. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Regions Rockstar has shown: **Vice City**, the **Leonida Keys**, **Grassrivers**, **Port Gellhorn**, **Ambrosia** and **Mount Kalaga National Park**. [Rockstar: Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)
- {% badge "reported" %} Game Informer's September 29 cover story, built on developer interviews, says Rockstar aimed to recreate Florida's ecology and culture as closely as it could. [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/digital-issue/2026/09/29/the-grand-theft-auto-vi-digital-issue-is-now-live)

Rockstar hasn't published a full map or an official map size. It says a free printable cover-art poster and map of Leonida goes up on the official site on November 19. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) Region guide: [GTA 6 locations](/locations/).
