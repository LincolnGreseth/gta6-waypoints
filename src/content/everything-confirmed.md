## Release date and platforms

- {% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches **November 19, 2026** on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The date has moved twice: from the original fall 2025 window to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026. Rockstar said the extra time was to finish the game "with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/gta-6-release-delayed-november-2026-1236571679/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two reaffirmed November 19 in its May 21 and August 7, 2026 results. [Take-Two IR (May)](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fourth-2), [Take-Two IR (Aug)](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no PS4 or Xbox One version, and the PlayStation Store FAQ says it isn't playable on PS4. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The PlayStation Store lists the game as **PS5 Pro Enhanced**. Sony hasn't said what the enhancements are. The Xbox Store listing is tagged "Optimized for Xbox Series X|S." [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/), [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9p3h4968grsm)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two reports its next results on Monday, November 9, 2026 after US markets close, with a call at 4:30 p.m. ET. It's the last scheduled investor update before launch. [Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-report-second-quarter-fiscal-8)

Unlock times by region haven't been announced. Our expected times for 15 store regions, labelled Unverified, are on [GTA 6 unlock times](/release-date-times/), and we'll update them as soon as Rockstar or the stores post official ones.

## Price and editions

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Standard Edition: $79.99.** **Ultimate Edition: $99.99.** [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Ultimate Edition adds 16 bonus items, including vehicles, weapons, outfits and mod shops. Rockstar names the '95 Grotti Cheetah and the Rideout Customs Mod Shop as examples. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately at any time on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} There's no physical Ultimate Edition. Physical Standard owners can add the upgrade digitally. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies contain a download code. "A disc will not be included in the box." [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Full breakdown and which one to buy: [GTA 6 editions compared](/editions/).

## Pre-orders, pre-load and bonuses

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Pre-orders opened at midnight local time on **June 25, 2026** on the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and retailers. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Vintage Vice City Pack:** included with pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before **November 20, 2026**. It has a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Digital pre-orders include **one free month of GTA+**, redeemable right away. It covers GTA Online perks and the GTA+ Games Library, which includes GTA V. On PlayStation it must be claimed by March 31, 2027. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Pre-load starts at local midnight on November 12, 2026.** Codes redeemed before launch can pre-load from that point too. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies ship and are available for store pickup from November 12. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation codes are region-locked; Xbox codes aren't. Rockstar recommends buying from a retailer in the same country as your console account. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Story and characters

- {% badge "confirmed" %} You play as **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos**. In Rockstar's words, "an easy score goes wrong" and the pair end up in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Supporting characters profiled by Rockstar: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. [Rockstar: Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} You can switch between Jason and Lucia, as shown in the Extended Look. [Official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tJbzMqJGH4k), [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The October 15 launch trailer shows Lucia wearing a monitoring ankle bracelet and Jason talking about life after the military. [Launch trailer](https://www.rockstargames.com/videos/76orsa95), [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/16/rockstar-releases-the-gta-6-launch-trailer-ahead-of-the-november-19-console-release/)

Character profiles: [GTA 6 characters](/characters/).

## Setting: Vice City and Leonida

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The game is set in the fictional US state of **Leonida**, centred on **Vice City**. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Regions Rockstar has shown: **Vice City**, the **Leonida Keys**, **Grassrivers**, **Port Gellhorn**, **Ambrosia** and **Mount Kalaga National Park**. [Rockstar: Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)
- {% badge "reported" %} Game Informer's September 29 cover story, built on developer interviews, says Rockstar aimed to recreate Florida's ecology and culture as closely as it could. [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/digital-issue/2026/09/29/the-grand-theft-auto-vi-digital-issue-is-now-live)

Rockstar hasn't published a full map or an official map size. Region guide: [GTA 6 locations](/locations/).

## Gameplay Rockstar has shown

Everything in this first list appears in Rockstar's own videos: the 26-minute Extended Look (August 27) and the launch trailer (October 15).

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Extended Look premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET, then on Rockstar's YouTube channel at 9 p.m. ET. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-extended-look-will-premier-exclusively-on-netflix-as-fans-beg-for-trailer-3-2000722148)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Cover-based shooting, with peek-and-shoot behind walls. [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/), [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-extended-look-everything-revealed)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Car and dirt-bike racing. [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/), [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-extended-look-everything-revealed)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Robbing convenience stores, gas stations and phone shops. [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Two ways to steal a car: quietly with a shim, or fast and loud by smashing the window. [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Side activities on screen: kayaking, parasailing, basketball, scuba diving and a bench-press gym minigame. [SVG](https://www.svg.com/2246094/gta-6-extended-look-gameplay-rockstar-confirmed/), [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-extended-look-everything-revealed)

These come from interviews with Rockstar staff. They're solid, but we label them Reported until we see them in an official Rockstar post.

- {% badge "reported" %} Diet, exercise and sleep change how Jason and Lucia look. Rockstar to Dazed, August 2026. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/gta-6-guide/)
- {% badge "reported" %} More activities named in Game Informer's cover story: zoo visits, off-road racing, wrestling, pool and mini-golf. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/gta-6-guide/)
- {% badge "reported" %} An expanded weather system and a dedicated team writing pedestrian dialogue. Game Informer, September 2026. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/gta-6-guide/)
- {% badge "reported" %} No microtransactions and no generative AI in the single-player game, per Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson on the Kinda Funny Gamescast. [Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/news/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-launches-without-microtransactions/)

## GTA Online

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's pre-order press release describes GTA 6 as "a single-player experience" and doesn't mention an online mode. The PlayStation Store FAQ says the same, and the Xbox Store lists "Single player." [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/), [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-online-looks-unlikely-for-launch-as-rockstar-refers-to-the-game-as-a-single-player-experience/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The ESRB listing includes In-Game Purchases. Rockstar hasn't said what those will be. [ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Rockstar hasn't announced when, or in what form, online play arrives.

## PC version

- {% badge "confirmed" %} No PC version has been announced. Rockstar's platform page covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "reported" %} Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told Bloomberg in May 2026 that "Rockstar always starts on console." [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/take-two-ceo-says-gta-6-isnt-coming-to-pc-day-one-because-console-players-are-the-core-consumer-but-the-real-reason-is-obvious)
- {% badge "reported" %} Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball posted on October 7 that GTA 6 "will not be streaming exclusively or to PC." [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/xbox-confirms-gta-6-wont-stream-on-pc-3416221/)
- {% badge "reported" %} GamesBeat reported on October 13, citing four anonymous sources, that a PC version is planned for early 2027. Rockstar didn't comment. [Dexerto, summarising GamesBeat](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-releasing-on-pc-much-earlier-than-expected-report-3418569/)
