## When does GTA 6 come out?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar set the date in November 2025 and repeated it in its June 24, 2026 pre-order announcement. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

Key dates and the delay history: [GTA 6 release date and times](/release-date-times/).

## How much does GTA 6 cost?

{% badge "confirmed" %} The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 in the US. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Ultimate adds 16 bonus items and is digital only. Standard owners can buy the upgrade later. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Which one to buy: [GTA 6 editions compared](/editions/).

## When can I pre-load GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Pre-load is live. It opened on November 10, 2026: at local midnight on PS5 and at 00:00 GMT on Xbox. Code-in-box copies can activate the code and pre-load as soon as they're received. [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10), [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 10](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)

## What time does GTA 6 unlock?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar: the game "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. New Zealand goes first. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)

{% badge "unverified" %} Rockstar hasn't said how that works in countries with several time zones. The US Xbox Store's page data points to one US unlock at midnight Eastern (9 PM Pacific on November 18), but that's not an announcement, so we show each US zone at its own midnight for now. See [all 15 regions and the time where you are](/release-date-times/#all-15-regions).

## Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. Its platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "reported" %} Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball posted that GTA 6 "will not be streaming exclusively or to PC." [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/xbox-confirms-gta-6-wont-stream-on-pc-3416221/)

Every statement so far, dated: [GTA 6 PC release](/pc-release/).

## Does GTA 6 have GTA Online at launch?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's press release describes GTA 6 as "a single-player experience" and doesn't mention an online mode. The PlayStation Store FAQ uses the same wording. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Rockstar hasn't announced when, or in what form, online play arrives.

## Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

{% badge "confirmed" %} There's no native PS4 or Xbox One version. Rockstar's platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1am ET on November 19. You still buy the game separately, and the cloud version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings. From November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without Game Pass. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/)

## Does the physical version come on a disc?

{% badge "confirmed" %} No. Physical copies contain a download code: "A disc will not be included in the box." Code-in-box copies are at retailers from November 12, 2026, and you can pre-load as soon as you activate the code. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation codes are region-locked; Xbox codes aren't. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Is GTA 6 enhanced for PS5 Pro?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Yes. Rockstar says PS5 Pro "offers breathtaking visuals, with improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro." [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)

## What frame rate does GTA 6 run at?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar says the game is "targeting 30 frames per second" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It hasn't announced a 60fps or performance mode. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)

## What radio stations are in GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar revealed the first six stations on October 8, 2026: Cocoteo FM, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM, Dirty South Classics and Back Country Radio. [Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music)
