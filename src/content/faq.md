## When does GTA 6 come out?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar set the date in November 2025 and repeated it in its June 24, 2026 pre-order announcement. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

Key dates and the delay history: [GTA 6 release date and times](/release-date-times/).

## How much does GTA 6 cost?

{% badge "confirmed" %} The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 in the US. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Ultimate adds 16 bonus items and is digital only. Standard owners can buy the upgrade later. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Which one to buy: [GTA 6 editions compared](/editions/).

## When can I pre-load GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Digital pre-load starts at local midnight on November 12, 2026. Codes from physical copies redeemed before launch can pre-load from then too. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## What time does GTA 6 unlock?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar and the stores haven't announced unlock times for November 19. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "unverified" %} We expect local midnight on November 19 in each store region, so New Zealand goes first and US West last, 21 hours later. That's our assumption, not an announcement. See [all 15 regions and the time where you are](/release-date-times/#all-15-regions).

## Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. Its platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "reported" %} Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball posted that GTA 6 "will not be streaming exclusively or to PC." [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/xbox-confirms-gta-6-wont-stream-on-pc-3416221/)

Every statement so far, dated: [GTA 6 PC release](/pc-release/).

## Does GTA 6 have GTA Online at launch?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's press release describes GTA 6 as "a single-player experience" and doesn't mention an online mode. The PlayStation Store FAQ uses the same wording. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Rockstar hasn't announced when, or in what form, online play arrives.

## Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

{% badge "confirmed" %} No. Rockstar's platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Does the physical version come on a disc?

{% badge "confirmed" %} No. Physical copies contain a download code: "A disc will not be included in the box." They ship and are available for store pickup from November 12, 2026. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

{% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation codes are region-locked; Xbox codes aren't. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Is GTA 6 enhanced for PS5 Pro?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Yes. The PlayStation Store lists GTA 6 as PS5 Pro Enhanced. [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Sony and Rockstar haven't said what the enhancements are.

## What radio stations are in GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar revealed the first six stations on October 8, 2026: Cocoteo FM, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM, Dirty South Classics and Back Country Radio. [Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Justice composed the official score, and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 original tracks) releases November 19. [Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music)

Rockstar hasn't named the rest of the stations yet.

## What is GTA 6 rated?

{% badge "confirmed" %} The ESRB rated GTA 6 M (Mature 17+), with In-Game Purchases listed as an interactive element. [ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Rockstar hasn't announced the file size or required storage. Any figure you've seen shared online is not official. It's on our [not confirmed yet list](/everything-confirmed/#not-confirmed-yet).

## What do I get for pre-ordering GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} The Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## What's the difference between Standard and Ultimate?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Ultimate adds 16 bonus items, including vehicles, weapons, outfits and mod shops. There's no physical Ultimate, and Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions) Full comparison: [GTA 6 editions](/editions/).

## When does the Vintage Vice City Pack offer end?

{% badge "confirmed" %} It comes with pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before November 20, 2026. Buy on November 20 or later and you don't get it. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

## Do I need internet to play GTA 6?

{% badge "confirmed" %} Both store listings say "certain game features, content, services, or functions may require internet connection" and a Rockstar Games account. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/), [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9p3h4968grsm)

Rockstar hasn't said whether the single-player game can be played fully offline. Physical copies are a download code, so you need a connection to install the game either way.
