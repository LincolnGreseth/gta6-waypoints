## Controllers

### PS5

**{% aff "dualsense" %}DualSense Wireless Controller{% endaff %}**, the controller that comes with every PS5.

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Haptic feedback from dual actuators in place of traditional rumble motors, and adaptive triggers that change resistance to match what's happening in a game. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-wireless-controller/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Built-in microphone, built-in battery and a USB-C port. [PlayStation Direct](https://direct.playstation.com/en-us/buy-accessories/dualsense-wireless-controller)

**{% aff "dualsense-edge" %}DualSense Edge Wireless Controller{% endaff %}**, Sony's pro controller.

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Swappable back buttons (half-dome and lever styles included) and interchangeable stick caps (standard, high dome and low dome). [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-edge-wireless-controller/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Remap buttons, adjust stick and trigger settings, and save profiles you can switch between mid-game. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/dualsense-edge-wireless-controller/)

### Xbox Series X|S

**{% aff "xbox-controller" %}Xbox Wireless Controller{% endaff %}**, the standard Xbox pad.

- {% badge "confirmed" %} A dedicated Share button for screenshots and clips, Bluetooth, and a 3.5mm headset jack. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/controllers/xbox-wireless-controller)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Runs on AA batteries, up to 40 hours under Microsoft's test conditions. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/controllers/xbox-wireless-controller)

**{% aff "xbox-elite" %}Xbox Elite Wireless Controller Series 2{% endaff %}**, Microsoft's pro controller.

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Four swappable paddles and thumbstick tension adjustable across three levels. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/controllers/elite-wireless-controller-series-2)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Stores three custom profiles plus a default, and lasts up to 40 hours per charge. The Core (white) model doesn't include the carrying case. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/controllers/elite-wireless-controller-series-2)

## Headsets

Rockstar has revealed six radio stations so far, and an original score by Justice. Details are on [everything confirmed](/everything-confirmed/#music-and-radio).

**{% aff "pulse-elite" %}PULSE Elite Wireless Headset{% endaff %}** (PS5)

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Planar magnetic drivers and a retractable boom mic with AI noise rejection. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/pulse-elite-wireless-headset/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Up to 30 hours of battery, and a 10-minute charge gives about 2 hours. Connects over PlayStation Link (USB adapter included) and Bluetooth at the same time. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/accessories/pulse-elite-wireless-headset/)

**{% aff "xbox-headset" %}Xbox Wireless Headset{% endaff %}** (Xbox Series X|S)

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Up to 20 hours per charge; 30 minutes of charging gives about 4 hours. Bluetooth 5.3, so it can pair with your phone and console at once. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/headsets/xbox-wireless-headset)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Supports Dolby Atmos, Windows Sonic and DTS Headphone:X, with voice isolation, auto-mute and chat-balance dials on the earcups. [Xbox](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/accessories/headsets/xbox-wireless-headset)

## Capture: recording your runs

Start with what your console already does. You only need a capture card for long, high-quality recordings or streaming from a PC or Mac.

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **PS5:** save recent gameplay from the last 15 seconds up to 1 hour, or record up to 1 hour of new gameplay. 4K recording works with a compatible display. [PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/capture-ps5-gameplay-screenshots/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Xbox Series X|S:** the Share button on the controller records clips and screenshots, and you can record to external storage. Some games block capture. [Xbox Support](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/friends-social-activity/share-socialize/capture-game-clips-and-screenshots)

**{% aff "elgato-4ks" %}Elgato Game Capture 4K S{% endaff %}**

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Captures up to 4K60, 1440p60 or 1080p120, with passthrough up to 4K60, 1440p120 or 1080p240 and VRR. Elgato says it works with PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Elgato](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/game-capture-4k-s)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Connects over USB-C and needs Windows 11 or later, macOS 14.2 or later, or an iPad with USB-C. HDR capture is Windows only. [Elgato](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/game-capture-4k-s)

**{% aff "elgato-4kx" %}Elgato Game Capture 4K X{% endaff %}**

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Capture and passthrough up to 4K144 over HDMI 2.1, with VRR passthrough. Elgato lists it as recommended for PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X. [Elgato](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/game-capture-4k-x)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Elgato recommends a USB-C 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps) port; a slower port works at reduced resolution. HDR capture is Windows only. [Elgato](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/game-capture-4k-x)
