## Adding storage: what the console makers say

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **PS5:** add a PCIe Gen4 x4 M.2 NVMe SSD (M key), 250 GB to 8 TB, with 5,500 MB/s sequential read or faster recommended. A heatsink or other cooling is required, and SATA drives aren't supported. [PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5-install-m2-ssd/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Xbox Series X|S:** the Storage Expansion Card matches the internal SSD's performance and works in both consoles with no setup. USB drives can store and play older Xbox games, but not games optimized for Series X|S. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/09/24/xbox-series-x-and-xbox-series-s-custom-storage-solution-primer/)
