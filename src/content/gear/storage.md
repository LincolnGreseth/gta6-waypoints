## The official file size

{% badge "confirmed" %} "The pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on your device." [Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10)

{% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation's own pre-load guide tells PS5 owners to clear "a minimum of 212GB" of storage. [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)

Store listings can show a slightly different number because some use decimal gigabytes and some binary. Go by the number your console shows when you start the download.

## Check your free space

- **PS5:** Settings → Storage → Console Storage shows free space at the top.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Settings → System → Storage devices.

A launch-day update will need extra room on top of the pre-load, so leave some headroom. Rockstar hasn't said how big any update will be.

## Adding storage: what the console makers say

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **PS5:** add a {% aff "ps5-m2-ssd" %}PCIe Gen4 M.2 NVMe SSD{% endaff %}, specifically a PCIe Gen4 x4 M.2 NVMe SSD (M key), 250 GB to 8 TB, with 5,500 MB/s sequential read or faster recommended. A heatsink or other cooling is required, and SATA drives aren't supported. [PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5-install-m2-ssd/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **Xbox Series X|S:** the {% aff "xbox-expansion-card" %}Storage Expansion Card{% endaff %} matches the internal SSD's performance and works in both consoles with no setup. USB drives can store and play older Xbox games, but not games optimized for Series X|S. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/09/24/xbox-series-x-and-xbox-series-s-custom-storage-solution-primer/)
