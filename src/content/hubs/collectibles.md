## What you'll find here

- Every collectible category, with the total count and your completion percentage
- A page per category with a table of locations, map links, tips and difficulty
- Rewards for collecting each set, once we've earned them

## Why there's nothing listed yet

We only publish real in-game names. Category pages go up as soon as we've confirmed the names and counts in our own play, not before. Guessing names now would just mean broken links later.

## Where they'll link

Each collectible shows up as a pin on the [interactive map](/map/), counts toward the [100% checklist](/100-percent/), and links to any [trophy or achievement](/trophies/) it unlocks.
