## What the map will do

- Filter pins by type: collectibles, vehicles, points of interest and heists
- Search by name, with clusters that split apart as you zoom
- Click any pin for details, a screenshot and a link to the full guide
- Tick off what you've found, saved on your device

## Confirmed so far

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Six regions shown by Rockstar: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. [Rockstar: Only in Leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar adds a free printable map of Leonida to the official GTA VI site on November 19. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10)
- {% badge "reported" %} Rockstar told Edge the landmass is "nearly as large as the playable maps of GTAIV, GTAV and RDR2 combined." [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/?p=317716)

## How pins get on the map

We play with capture running and log every find with its location, steps and a clip. A pin is published once it's verified, and anything we haven't retested is marked **Unverified — testing**. We don't copy pins from other sites and we don't use leaked files.
