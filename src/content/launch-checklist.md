faq:
  - q: "When can I pre-load GTA 6?"
    a: "Now. Pre-load opened on November 10, 2026: at local midnight on PS5 and at 00:00 GMT on Xbox. Codes from physical copies can pre-load as soon as they're redeemed."
  - q: "What time does GTA 6 unlock?"
    a: "At midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions, per Rockstar Support. New Zealand goes first. Rockstar hasn't said how countries with several time zones (the US, Australia, Brazil) unlock, so those stay Unverified on our unlock times page."
  - q: "Do I need to pre-order to get the bonus?"
    a: "No. The Vintage Vice City Pack comes with pre-orders and with purchases of either edition made before November 20, 2026."
  - q: "Does the physical version come with a disc?"
    a: "No. The box contains a download code, so you'll still need to download the game. Code-in-box copies are at retailers from November 12, and the code can pre-load as soon as you redeem it."
  - q: "How much storage does GTA 6 need?"
    a: "Up to 212 GB, depending on your console, per Rockstar. PlayStation recommends clearing at least 212 GB on a PS5."
  - q: "Do physical copies get the free month of GTA+?"
    a: "No. Rockstar lists the GTA+ month with digital pre-orders only. Physical pre-orders include the game code and the Vintage Vice City Pack."
