## What Rockstar announced

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on September 17, 2026. It was made in partnership with Atlantic Records. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The album has 34 original tracks and comes out November 19, the same day as the game. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The first six singles are streaming now; Rockstar's post says they were available on announcement day. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19))

## Prices and editions

| Edition | Price | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| CD | $19.98 | Audio CD and booklet in a jewel case |
| Vinyl | $49.98 | Two transparent magenta discs with blue splatter |
| Limited-edition vinyl | $124.98 | Two transparent magenta discs filled with blue liquid |

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Those are the prices and contents on the official album store. ([official store: CD](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/products/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-cd), [vinyl](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/products/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-vinyl), [limited-edition vinyl](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/products/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-limited-edition-vinyl))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} All three physical editions ship from November 20, 2026. ([official store](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/products/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-vinyl))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The limited-edition vinyl shows as sold out on the official store as of October 26. ([official store](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/products/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-limited-edition-vinyl))

## Store and retailer exclusives

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The liquid-filled vinyl, the splatter vinyl and the CD jewel case are exclusive to the official album store. Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Target and other retailers get their own vinyl colorways with new art. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19))
- {% badge "reported" %} GTA BOOM reports the vinyl variants come with their own posters. Rockstar's post doesn't mention posters. ([GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/rockstar-is-pressing-the-complete-gta-6-soundtrack-to-vinyl-for-125-7c6c))
