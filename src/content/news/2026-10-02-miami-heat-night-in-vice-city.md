## What's happening

- {% badge "reported" %} The Miami Heat host "A Night in Vice City" on November 18, 2026, the night before launch. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-had-a-really-busy-week-2000739409), [Miami HEAT on X](https://x.com/MiamiHEAT/status/2106039997480697918))
- {% badge "reported" %} Kotaku reports that Rockstar is teaming up with the Heat to host the takeover night. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-had-a-really-busy-week-2000739409))
- {% badge "reported" %} The Heat's event page lists tip-off at 7:00 p.m. ET at Kaseya Center. ([Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/uniforms/2627-heat-vice-city-uniform-collection))
- {% badge "reported" %} The same page says Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands open at select concessions inside the arena. ([Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/uniforms/2627-heat-vice-city-uniform-collection))

## The court, uniforms and merch

- {% badge "reported" %} The arena gets a new GTA 6-inspired court for the game, according to Kotaku. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-had-a-really-busy-week-2000739409), [Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/uniforms/2627-heat-vice-city-uniform-collection))
- {% badge "reported" %} Heat players wear new Vice City uniforms, Kotaku reports. The Heat haven't shown the design yet. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-had-a-really-busy-week-2000739409), [Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/uniforms/2627-heat-vice-city-uniform-collection))
- {% badge "reported" %} The Heat are selling new GTA 6 merch and shirts, according to Kotaku. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-had-a-really-busy-week-2000739409))

## The game itself

{% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches the next day, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
