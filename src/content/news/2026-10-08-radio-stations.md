{% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar revealed the first six stations and their on-air hosts on October 8, 2026. ([Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music), [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o3982oa93a23k4/the-music-of-grand-theft-auto-vi-in-game-radio-stations))

## The six stations

The on-air host names are Rockstar's own. The "Reported as" column is the real artists behind them, according to [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/gta-6-radio-stations-be-hosted-robyn-palms-trax-bad-bunny-more). Rockstar's text doesn't name them.

| Station | Sound | On-air hosts {% badge "confirmed" %} | Reported as {% badge "reported" %} |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cocoteo FM | Latin | El Martillo and La Gata | Reports conflict, so we've left this out |
| AfroBank FM | Afrobeat, amapiano, 3-step | Burna and Palmsy | Burna Boy, Palms Trax |
| The Chamber 106.6 | Classic metal | DJ Kerry and DJ Tom | Kerry King, Tom Araya |
| Flash FM | Pop | Robyn and Alex | Robyn, Alex Consani |
| Dirty South Classics | Regional rap and hip-hop | Trick and Trina | Trick Daddy, Trina |
| Back Country Radio | Country | MW and Delta Dawne | Morgan Wallen, Lana Del Rey |

## Podcasts and headphones

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Podcasts are on demand for the first time in the series. ([Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music), [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/10/08/you-can-preview-6-of-the-radio-stations-in-grand-theft-auto-vi-right-now-plus-new))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} You can listen to radio and podcasts on foot through headphones or earbuds connected to the in-game phone. ([Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music))

## More stations to come

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar says the full lineup runs "from Classic Rock to Talk Radio". Those other stations haven't been named yet. ([Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music))

We'll add them here when Rockstar names them. Every other official detail is on [everything confirmed about GTA 6](/everything-confirmed/).
