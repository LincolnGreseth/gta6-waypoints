## What happened

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar released the launch trailer on October 15, 2026. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer), [official video](https://www.rockstargames.com/videos/76orsa95))

## What the trailer shows

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Lucia wears a monitoring ankle bracelet. ([official video](https://www.rockstargames.com/videos/76orsa95), [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/16/rockstar-releases-the-gta-6-launch-trailer-ahead-of-the-november-19-console-release/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Jason talks about life after the military. ([official video](https://www.rockstargames.com/videos/76orsa95), [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/16/rockstar-releases-the-gta-6-launch-trailer-ahead-of-the-november-19-console-release/))

## The music

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Justice composed the official score. Their single "Flower in a Hurricane", with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, debuted with the launch trailer. ([Rockstar: Music of GTA VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music))

{% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
