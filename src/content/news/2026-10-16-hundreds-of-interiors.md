## What Rockstar said

- {% badge "reported" %} Bloomberg's Jason Schreier attended a studio demo at Rockstar North. Bloomberg's report was published on October 16, 2026. ([TalkEsport, citing Bloomberg](https://www.talkesport.com/news/gta-6-hundreds-of-interiors/))
- {% badge "reported" %} During the demo, a Rockstar developer said there are "hundreds" of small interiors you can walk into and explore. ([TalkEsport, citing Bloomberg](https://www.talkesport.com/news/gta-6-hundreds-of-interiors/))

## Is there an exact number?

- {% badge "reported" %} No. "Hundreds" is the most precise figure Rockstar has given. It hasn't published a count. ([TalkEsport, citing Bloomberg](https://www.talkesport.com/news/gta-6-hundreds-of-interiors/))
- {% badge "reported" %} TalkEsport says lists online claiming 700 or more enterable buildings are fan speculation, not a Rockstar figure. ([TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/news/gta-6-hundreds-of-interiors/))
- {% badge "reported" %} Rockstar hasn't said every building can be entered. ([TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/news/gta-6-hundreds-of-interiors/))

We'll swap in an official number if Rockstar or Take-Two shares one. Until then, treat any specific count you see as a guess.
