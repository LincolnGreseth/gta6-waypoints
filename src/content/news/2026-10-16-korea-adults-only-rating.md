## The Korean rating

- {% badge "reported" %} South Korea's Game Content Rating Board held a rating meeting on October 16, 2026, and rated GTA 6 adults-only, according to Sports Kyunghyang. ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202610191644007))
- {% badge "reported" %} Sports Kyunghyang published the news on October 19. The report doesn't give a Korean age number. ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202610191644007))

## The US rating

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The ESRB rates Grand Theft Auto VI M (Mature 17+) on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The ESRB content descriptors are Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol. ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The ESRB listing also notes In-Game Purchases. Rockstar hasn't said what those will be. ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/))

## What it means

An adults-only rating in Korea means the game is restricted to adult players there. It doesn't change the November 19 launch date.

{% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
