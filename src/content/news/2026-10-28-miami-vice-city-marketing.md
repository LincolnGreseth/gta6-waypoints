## The county deal

- {% badge "reported" %} Miami-Dade County's press release describes a package "valued at $3.9 million": an agreement with Rockstar Games plus ad space bought through the companies that run Miami International Airport and county transit advertising. ([Miami-Dade County](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/release.page?Mduid_release=rel1793224106473569))
- {% badge "reported" %} It covers themed lighting at the airport and PortMiami, displays on the walkways from the airport garages to the terminals, and wraps on selected traffic-signal cabinets. ([Miami-Dade County](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/release.page?Mduid_release=rel1793224106473569))
- {% badge "reported" %} The total includes the earlier $975,000 "Welcome to Vice City" rooftop display at Kaseya Center; the county says that money goes to a park at Dan Paul Plaza, and Rockstar covers production and installation. ([Miami-Dade County](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/release.page?Mduid_release=rel1793224106473569))
- {% badge "reported" %} WLRN reports some county commissioners objected that the deal didn't go to a full commission vote. ([WLRN](https://www.wlrn.org/government-politics/2026-10-29/miami-dade-announces-4-million-gta-vi-marketing-agreement-with-rockstar))

We label this Reported: it's the county's announcement, and Rockstar hasn't posted about it.

## Burger Shot at the Heat game

- {% badge "reported" %} The Heat's "A Night in Vice City" page lists Burger Shot stands at sections 105, 120, 308 and 324 and Cluckin' Bell at section 325 for the November 18 game against the Bucks, 7 p.m. ET. ([Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/vicecity))
- {% badge "reported" %} The same page lists a Nike x Cactus Jack "Vice City" uniform, on sale November 18 at the game. The page calls it "a historic partnership" but doesn't name Rockstar. ([Miami HEAT](https://www.nba.com/heat/vicecity))
