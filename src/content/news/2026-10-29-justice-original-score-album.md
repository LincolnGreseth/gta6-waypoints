## The album

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Composed by "Grammy Award winners Justice," the French duo of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} It's an adaptive score: Rockstar says the compositions are "dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world". ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Out December 11, 2026 on all major streaming services, CD and vinyl, with 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks across all formats. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Includes "Flower in a Hurricane" feat. Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, and the new single "Pusher" feat. Gesaffelstein. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice))

## Not to be confused with The Album

This is separate from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the 34-track soundtrack of original songs out November 19. See [the soundtrack album](/news/2026-09-17-gta-6-soundtrack-album/).
