## What was reported

- {% badge "reported" %} Per MP1st, Rockstar told Edge the landmass, including Vice City and surrounding areas, is "nearly as large as the playable maps of GTAIV, GTAV and RDR2 combined." ([MP1st](https://mp1st.com/?p=317716))
- {% badge "reported" %} The interview is part of Edge's GTA 6 cover feature in issue 430. ([GamesRadar+](https://gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-exclusive-leads-edge-430-check-out-the-16-page-cover-feature-that-includes-interviews-with-rockstar-bespoke-screenshots-and-more-this-week))

This is an interview comparison, not a measured size. We haven't read the magazine itself, so the label stays Reported.
