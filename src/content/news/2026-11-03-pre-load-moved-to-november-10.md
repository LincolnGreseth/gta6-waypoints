## What Rockstar announced

- {% badge "confirmed" %} "Digital pre-loading on PlayStation 5 will begin on Tuesday, November 10 at midnight local time in your respective time zone." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "On XBOX Series X|S, pre-loading begins globally on Tuesday, November 10 at 12 a.m. GMT." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "Code-in-box versions for both consoles should be available from retailers beginning November 12." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "The pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on your device." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} On November 19, Rockstar adds a free printable cover-art poster and map of Leonida to the official site, with a physical version at the Rockstar Store shortly after launch. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))

## What changed

Rockstar's support page previously gave November 12 as the pre-load date. It now reads November 10, matching the post. ([Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)) Pre-load opened as announced; see [pre-load is live](/news/2026-11-10-gta-6-pre-load-live-212gb-30fps/).
