## What was said

- {% badge "reported" %} On the call, Zelnick said: "So right now, Rockstar has completed the development of GTA VI." This is from a third-party transcript of the call, not a Take-Two document. ([Singju Post transcript](https://singjupost.com/?p=65173))
- {% badge "reported" %} Push Square and others reported this as GTA 6 having "gone gold", the industry term for a finished release build. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-wont-have-any-last-minute-delays-as-its-officially-gone-gold))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two's written release says the launch "is ten days away" and doesn't mention going gold. ([Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4))

## What it means for you

Pre-load opened the next day, on November 10 ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now)), and Rockstar's release date hasn't moved. A day-one update is still possible; Rockstar hasn't said either way. We'll label this Confirmed if Rockstar or Take-Two says it in its own published words.
