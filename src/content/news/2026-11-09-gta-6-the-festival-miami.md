## The event

- {% badge "confirmed" %} November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood, Miami, "to celebrate the launch of GTAVI." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "Presented in partnership with PlayStation and produced by the creators of III Points," with two stages. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's lineup: Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo; Cardi B; Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso; Justice (DJ set); Metro Boomin; Nick León B2B DJ Python; Prospa B2B Skream; Rauw & Friends; Suero; Tainy; The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set). ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/o39o87a435a25a/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-festival))

{% badge "confirmed" %} Tickets were sold through a DICE pre-sale lottery, with the sale on Wednesday, November 11. The lottery has closed; check Rockstar's post for any later release.
