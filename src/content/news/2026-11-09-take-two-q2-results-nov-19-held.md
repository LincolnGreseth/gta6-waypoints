## What Take-Two said

- {% badge "confirmed" %} CEO Strauss Zelnick: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company." ([Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two reiterated its fiscal 2027 Net Bookings outlook of $8 to $8.2 billion and gave its first outlook for the October–December quarter, the one GTA 6 launches in. ([Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The release lists Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026. ([Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4))

No pre-order count was published. Figures you see quoted come from data firms, not Take-Two.
