## Pre-load: when and how

- {% badge "confirmed" %} "Pre-loading is now available for everyone who has pre-ordered Grand Theft Auto VI digitally." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} On PS5 it opened at midnight local time on Tuesday, November 10. On Xbox Series X|S it opened worldwide at 12 a.m. GMT the same day. ([Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Bought a boxed copy? It holds a download code, not a disc. Code-in-box copies are at retailers from November 12, and you can activate the code and pre-load as soon as you have it. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))

## How much space you need

{% badge "confirmed" %} "The pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on your device." ([Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10)) PlayStation's own pre-load guide tells PS5 owners to clear at least 212 GB. ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/))

Short on space? Our [GTA 6 storage page](/gear/storage/) covers how to check and what to add.

## 30fps, and what PS5 Pro adds

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar says the game is "targeting 30 frames per second" on current consoles while keeping the density and detail of Leonida. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} PS5 Pro gets "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro." ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} On PS5, Rockstar also lists DualSense haptic feedback (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio and improved load times. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))

Rockstar hasn't mentioned a 60fps or performance mode.

## Xbox One and the cloud

{% badge "confirmed" %} The game is also on Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings, from 1am ET on November 19. Details: [GTA 6 on Xbox Cloud Gaming](/news/2026-11-10-gta-6-xbox-cloud-gaming-xbox-one/).
