## The details

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, "starting at 1am ET." ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately." ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} "The game uses XBOX Series S graphics settings when cloud-streamed." ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} From November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription. Xbox doesn't list the markets in the post. ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar lists cloud play on "XBOX Series X|S and XBOX One consoles" and warns that connection speed can affect performance in ways that wouldn't happen locally. ([Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now))

## What it isn't

This is console streaming, not a PC release. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version; we track that on [the PC page](/pc-release/). The 1am ET cloud start is separate from the console unlock, which is midnight local time ([unlock times](/release-date-times/)).
