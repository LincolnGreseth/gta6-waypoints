## What each company said

- {% badge "confirmed" %} PlayStation: "Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store." ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Xbox: "the most preordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store". ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two, a day earlier, said pre-orders are "continuing to reach unprecedented levels," without a figure. ([Take-Two IR](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-4))

## What we're not repeating

Data firms have published pre-order estimates. They're third-party estimates, not sales figures from Sony, Microsoft or Take-Two, so we don't list them.
