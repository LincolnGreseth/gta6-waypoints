## Sources and notes

- {% badge "confirmed" %} **ESRB:** M for Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol, with In-Game Purchases. ([ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/41627/grand-theft-auto-vi/))
- {% badge "confirmed" %} **USK:** no youth rating (18), with descriptors for drastic violence, drugs and criminal role models, for both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. ([USK](https://usk.de/en/?s=grand+theft+auto+vi))
- {% badge "reported" %} **CERO Z:** reported by GameBusiness.jp from the Japanese PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings. Neither CERO nor Rockstar has announced any differences in the Japanese version. ([GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/10/18/28364.html))
- {% badge "reported" %} **South Korea:** rated adults-only by the Game Content Rating Board (GCRB), which took over console reviews in November 2025. ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27299/gta-6-rated-adults-only-in-south-korea-following-private-board-review))
- {% badge "reported" %} **Australia:** R18+, as reported by TwistedVoxel on October 3. We couldn't open the Classification Board's page to check it ourselves.
