## What to do

Our advice, not Rockstar's:

1. Turn the console on and connect to the internet before your unlock time.
2. On PS5, open the game's tile, press Options and choose **Check for Update**. On Xbox, go to **My games & apps → Manage → Updates**.
3. Let any GTA 6 update finish before you start. If a new one appears at midnight, install that too.

Still downloading the base game? It needs up to 212 GB. See the [launch checklist](/launch-checklist/).
