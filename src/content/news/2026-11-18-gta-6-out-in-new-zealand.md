## What's official

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar says the game "will release midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar Support last updated that page on November 3 and hasn't added per-country times. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)
- {% badge "reported" %} New Zealand went first. VICE reported on November 18 that the game is out there, and Anadolu Agency reported the rollout starting in New Zealand. We haven't found a Rockstar post saying it's live. [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-new-zealand-worldwide-release-times/), [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/science-technology/gta-vi-global-rollout-begins-with-new-zealand-launch/4129238)

## When it unlocks where you are

Our [unlock times page](/release-date-times/) lists all 15 store regions, shows which ones are live now, and counts down to yours. Countries with several time zones (the US, Australia and Brazil) are marked with a * because Rockstar hasn't said how they unlock.

Other regions unlock earlier only because of time zones. Changing your console region to play sooner can break the store terms.

## Before you play

Let your console install the latest update before you start. Two pre-launch updates have been reported; see [which update to install](/news/2026-11-18-gta-6-day-one-updates/).
