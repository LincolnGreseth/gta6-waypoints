## The short answer

| Time zone | If it's one US unlock (reported) | If it's each zone's midnight |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern | 12:00 AM Thu, Nov 19 | 12:00 AM Thu, Nov 19 |
| Central | 11:00 PM Wed, Nov 18 | 12:00 AM Thu, Nov 19 |
| Mountain | 10:00 PM Wed, Nov 18 | 12:00 AM Thu, Nov 19 |
| Pacific | 9:00 PM Wed, Nov 18 | 12:00 AM Thu, Nov 19 |

If you're west of Eastern time, check your console from the earlier time.

## What's official

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar: "midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions." It doesn't say how that works in a country with several time zones. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming starts at 1 AM ET on November 19. That's separate from the console unlock. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/)

## What's reported

- {% badge "reported" %} VICE lists the US unlock as 12 AM ET on Thursday, or 9 PM PT on Wednesday. [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-new-zealand-worldwide-release-times/)
- {% badge "reported" %} The US Xbox Store page's data gives a release time of 05:00 UTC on November 19, which is midnight Eastern. That's store metadata, not an announcement, so we don't count it as official. [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9p3h4968grsm)

Until Rockstar or the stores say more, our [unlock times page](/release-date-times/) keeps the four US zones marked Unverified.
