GTA 6 Waypoints is an independent fan site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related marks are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.

## Trademarks

Grand Theft Auto VI, Grand Theft Auto, GTA, Rockstar Games and related names and marks belong to their owners, including Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. We use them only to describe the game we're writing about. We don't use Rockstar's logos, fonts or official art as our branding, and we don't sell anything that uses GTA trademarks.

Other product names, brands and logos mentioned on this site belong to their owners.

## No leaks

We never publish, link to or describe leaked material: footage, files, builds or claims from unofficial "insiders". We also don't cover the leaks themselves. If you send us leaked material, we delete it.

## Accuracy

Game details change. Announcements get updated, patches change how things work, and early reports can turn out wrong. We try hard to be accurate:

- Every claim carries a label: Confirmed, Reported, Rumor or Unverified. Our [about page](/about/) explains what each one means.
- Every claim links to its source.
- Every page shows the date we last updated it.

Even so, we can't guarantee that everything is complete, current or error-free. Check the linked sources before you rely on anything, especially prices, dates and availability. If you spot an error, [let us know](/contact/).

## Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. Here's how they work:

- Every affiliate link is marked "(paid link)" next to it, and it carries `rel="sponsored"` so search engines know it's paid.
- If you buy something through one, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra.
- Commissions never change what we recommend or how we rank products. We only link to products that are relevant to the page.
- Pages with affiliate links show this disclosure near the top.

Programs we use:

- **Amazon Associates**, now.
- Other retailer and brand programs (for example SteelSeries or Razer) may be added later. We'll list them here when they are.

We don't link to cloud gaming services, because GTA 6 isn't announced for PC.
