## What's official

- {% badge "confirmed" %} No PC version has been announced. Rockstar's platform page covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar's June 24, 2026 pre-order announcement lists GTA 6 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, launching November 19, 2026. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi)

## What's been reported

- {% badge "reported" %} Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told Bloomberg in May 2026 that "Rockstar always starts on console." [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/take-two-ceo-says-gta-6-isnt-coming-to-pc-day-one-because-console-players-are-the-core-consumer-but-the-real-reason-is-obvious)
- {% badge "reported" %} Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball posted on October 7, 2026 that GTA 6 "will not be streaming exclusively or to PC." [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/xbox-confirms-gta-6-wont-stream-on-pc-3416221/)
- {% badge "reported" %} GamesBeat reported on October 13, 2026, citing four anonymous sources, that a PC version is planned for early 2027. Rockstar didn't comment. This is a single outlet's report, not an announcement. [Dexerto, summarising GamesBeat](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-releasing-on-pc-much-earlier-than-expected-report-3418569/)

Treat the GamesBeat report as a report until Rockstar says something. We won't move it to Confirmed without an official post.

## What this means if you only have a PC

You can't play GTA 6 on PC on November 19. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date, and Xbox says it won't stream to PC. If you want to play at launch, you'll need a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S.

We'll update this page the day Rockstar or Take-Two says anything new about PC.
