Everything on GTA 6 Waypoints stays free: the map, collectibles, trophies, the 100% checklist and every guide. Pro would sit on top for players who want to finish Grand Theft Auto VI faster.

## What Pro would add

- **Route planner.** Pick what you still need and get the shortest route around the map to collect it.
- **Video timestamps.** Jump straight to the second in our videos where each collectible or step appears.
- **Full databases.** Every vehicle, collectible and trophy, with filters and exports, synced across your devices.

## Why a waitlist

We're deciding whether to build it. If enough people join, we will, and waitlist members hear first. We'll only email you about Pro and the site.
