## All 15 regions

{% include "partials/unlock-table.njk" %}

Tap a region to make it yours. Your unlock follows your PlayStation or Xbox account region, not your device. Sony describes it as "a rolling midnight launch by country", starting at 12:00 AM NZDT in New Zealand ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/)).

{% badge "unverified" %} **The US question.** The US Xbox Store listing's page data gives a release time of 05:00 UTC on November 19, which is midnight Eastern (9:00 PM Pacific on Wednesday, November 18). That's store metadata, not an announcement, and neither Rockstar nor PlayStation has said the US unlocks all at once. If it does, US Central, Mountain and West unlock one to three hours before their own midnight. We'll change the table the moment an official source says so.

## Xbox Cloud Gaming (separate from the console unlock)

{% badge "confirmed" %} Streaming through Xbox Cloud Gaming starts at **1am ET on November 19** on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S. You still have to buy the game, the cloud version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings, and from November 11 players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without Game Pass. [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/)

<p class="spoiler"><strong>Spoiler warning.</strong> Because unlocks follow local midnight, players in New Zealand and Australia are playing many hours before Europe and the Americas. Mute keywords and stay off the subreddits on launch night if you want to go in fresh.</p>
