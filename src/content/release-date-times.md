## Release date

- {% badge "confirmed" %} GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar press release](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624539426/en/Rockstar-Games-Announces-Pre-Orders-for-Grand-Theft-Auto-VI)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} There's no PS4 or Xbox One version. Rockstar's platform page lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} No PC version has been announced. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions) More on the [PC release page](/pc-release/).

## Unlock times by region

Rockstar and the platform stores haven't announced official unlock times for November 19. Until they do, the countdown above runs to midnight in your own timezone.

We'll post the times for each region here as soon as Rockstar, the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store publishes them. It's on our [not confirmed yet list](/everything-confirmed/#not-confirmed-yet) until then.

## Pre-load

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Pre-load starts at local midnight on November 12, 2026, one week before launch. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Codes redeemed before launch, including the codes in physical boxes, can pre-load from that point too. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Rockstar hasn't announced the download size. Clear space before November 12 so the pre-load isn't stuck waiting on you.

## Physical copies

- {% badge "confirmed" %} Physical copies ship and are available for store pickup from November 12. [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The box contains a download code. "A disc will not be included in the box." [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)

Edition details are on [GTA 6 editions compared](/editions/).

## PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The PlayStation Store lists GTA 6 as PS5 Pro Enhanced. Its FAQ says the game releases "for PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro on November 19, 2026." [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} The Xbox Store listing carries the "Optimized for Xbox Series X|S" tag. [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9p3h4968grsm)

Sony and Rockstar haven't said what the PS5 Pro enhancements are.

| When | What changed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Original plan | A fall 2025 release window | {% badge "confirmed" %} |
| First delay | Moved to May 26, 2026 | {% badge "confirmed" %} |
| Nov 2025 | Delayed again to November 19, 2026 | {% badge "confirmed" %} |
| May 21, 2026 | Take-Two reaffirms November 19 in its earnings report | {% badge "confirmed" %} |
| Jun 24, 2026 | Pre-order press release repeats November 19 | {% badge "confirmed" %} |
| Aug 7, 2026 | Take-Two reaffirms November 19 in its Q1 results | {% badge "confirmed" %} |

- {% badge "confirmed" %} The release moved twice: from the original fall 2025 window to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026. [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/gta-6-release-delayed-november-2026-1236571679/)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Rockstar said the extra time was to finish the game "with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026)
- {% badge "confirmed" %} Take-Two reaffirmed the date in May 2026 and again on August 7, 2026. [Take-Two IR, May 21](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fourth-2), [Take-Two IR, Aug 7](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-first-6)
